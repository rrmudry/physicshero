About PhysicsHero.net

physicshero.net is the public repository and showcase for interactive physics models and web applications developed by R. Mudry.

All applications deployed here are sanitized, standalone versions adapted from classroom course materials—free of proprietary data, student information, and course-specific grading assets—shared openly for learners and the wider physics community.
